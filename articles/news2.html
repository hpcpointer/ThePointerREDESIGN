Robotics Season Is Underway

By The Pointer · 10/02/26

Robotics is welcoming students ready to take on the next challenge.

Robotics season is officially underway, and students interested in building, design or programming can become involved with the team.

New members are welcome to join. With a $25 entrance fee, students can contribute to building, designing or coding the robot for the team's next challenge.

Robotics is not limited to one specific skill. Building a competition robot requires students to work together, solve problems and figure out how different parts of a project can work as one.

Interested students can speak with Mr. Watkins in Room 124 for more information.
