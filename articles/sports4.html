Baseball Begins Winter Workouts

By The Pointer · 10/02/26

The baseball program is already preparing for the next season and looking for students ready to get involved.

While baseball season may still be ahead, the team is beginning its preparation early with winter workouts every Monday and Wednesday.

Winter workouts give interested players an opportunity to stay active, develop their skills and become familiar with the program before the regular season begins.

Students interested in joining or learning more about the program should speak with the coach in Room 216.

The program is encouraging students to get involved and help continue building baseball at High Point Central.
