Find Your Sound With Band

By The Pointer · 10/02/26

From the stands to community events, the band gives students a chance to perform and represent HPC.

Students interested in music have an opportunity to become involved with the High Point Central band program.

Band allows students to learn and perform an instrument while contributing to school events, athletic events and community performances.

For students who enjoy music, the program can also provide a way to perform as part of a larger group and build skills through regular practice and collaboration.

Students interested in learning more should see Mrs. Pender in the band room.
