Lady Bison Tennis Battles Through Competition

By Caden & Nevaeh · 10/02/26

Recent matches have brought challenges, but the tennis team continues to build experience with every game.

The Lady Bison tennis team has spent its recent matches facing strong competition while continuing to develop as a team.

Over the past three games, the players have competed through a difficult stretch. On Monday, the team battled through its individual matches. Tuesday brought a win for one doubles team, while Wednesday presented another challenge with only five players competing.

Despite the recent losses, the team has continued to use each match as an opportunity to improve. Competitive matches give players a chance to work on communication, confidence and decision-making while gaining experience against different opponents.

With the season continuing, every match provides another opportunity for the players to develop their skills and prepare for future competitions.

Students can support the Lady Bison by attending upcoming matches and showing their school spirit.
