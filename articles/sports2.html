Lady Bison Flag Football Starts Strong

By Cayden · 10/02/26

With a 1–1 record and standout performances already on the board, the Lady Bison are looking toward a promising season.

The Lady Bison flag football team kicked off its season on September 28, bringing another opportunity for students to compete and represent High Point Central on the field.

The team currently holds a 1–1 record and recently earned an 18–8 victory over Glen.

Senior #3 Amirah Herrera led the offense with three passing touchdowns and more than 120 combined rushing and passing yards. On defense, senior #2 Sarah Bryant recorded three interceptions.

The early season has also provided an opportunity for the program to continue growing. Students interested in joining can still become part of the team.

Flag football gives students an opportunity to compete, develop teamwork and stay involved with school athletics.

Students interested in joining should speak with Coach King in the auxiliary gym.
