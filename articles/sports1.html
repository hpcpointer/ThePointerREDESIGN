The Bison Are Back: Football Picks Up a Big Win

By Felix Elrod · 10/02/26

After a difficult stretch, the Bison are finding their momentum again.

The Bison football team has continued its season with a series of ups and downs, but the team recently gave fans something to celebrate with a 34–8 victory over the Ledford Panthers.

The season began with a loss to West Caldwell before the Bison responded with a win against the Southwest Cowboys. The team then faced losses against Central Davidson and Montgomery Central.

Rather than allowing the losing streak to define the season, the Bison returned against Ledford with a convincing win.

Senior Bison player Trussiah Covington, #2, described the game as “a must-win,” while quarterback Davohn Hightower said the team “bounced back with a better mindset.”

Beyond the scoreboard, the game represents another opportunity for the team to build confidence and establish its identity as the season continues.

Students can support the team by attending upcoming games, bringing friends and helping create an energetic environment in the stands.
