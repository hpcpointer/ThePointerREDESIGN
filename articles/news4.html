Beta Club Combines Academics With Community Service

By The Pointer · 10/02/26

Students can turn academic achievement into opportunities for leadership and service through Beta Club.

For students interested in combining academics with community involvement, Beta Club offers an opportunity to participate in a school organization focused on achievement and service.

Students with a 3.0 GPA or higher may be eligible to join. Beyond academic recognition, the organization gives students opportunities to participate in community service and contribute to their school and surrounding community.

Beta Club can also give members a chance to work with other academically motivated students while developing leadership and service experience outside of the classroom.

Students who are eligible and interested should look for a letter from Ms. Stafford with information about membership.
