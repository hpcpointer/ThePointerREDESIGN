Dive In: Swim Team Interest Meeting Set

By The Pointer · 10/02/26

Interested in making a splash this season? The swim team is looking for new athletes.

Students interested in swimming competitively at High Point Central will have an opportunity to learn more about the team at an upcoming interest meeting.

The meeting will take place on Wednesday, October 7, and will provide interested students with information about joining the team and the upcoming season.

Swimming gives students the opportunity to compete individually while also contributing to a larger team. For students looking for an alternative to traditional field sports, the swim team provides another way to compete and stay active.

Students interested in joining should attend the October 7 meeting.
