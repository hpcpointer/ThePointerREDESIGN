The invite is out, and the countdown begins.

By The Pointer · 09/28/26

High Point Central’s Homecoming Night is coming up on Friday, October 16, from 8–11 p.m., bringing this year’s Project B: House Party theme to life. Inspired by the atmosphere of a 2000s house party, the event will feature music, dancing and a night centered around students coming together for Homecoming. The theme takes inspiration from the 2012 film Project X, giving the event a throwback feel while keeping it as a school-sponsored celebration. Tickets are $15 for students and $25 for non-students. Students planning to attend should also be aware of the event's eligibility requirements. Students must have no OSS or ISS during the past school year.

Homecoming Court sign-ups will take place October 5–9. Students interested in running for Homecoming Court must have at least 85% attendance, have no OSS or ISS during the past school year, and have no more than one RS placement. For students, Homecoming is more than just a dance. It is one of the few nights during the school year when different parts of the student body come together in one place. From the court and decorations to the music and crowd, the atmosphere depends heavily on the students who show up. With the House Party theme setting the tone, students can expect a night built around music, friends and school spirit. October 16. 8–11 p.m. You know where to be.
