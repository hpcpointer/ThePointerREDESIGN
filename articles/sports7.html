Wrestling Tryouts Coming in November

By The Pointer · 10/02/26

Ready to test your discipline, endurance and determination? Wrestling season is approaching.

Students interested in joining the wrestling team will have the opportunity to try out beginning November 2.

Wrestling combines physical conditioning with technique, discipline and mental focus. Athletes also develop perseverance while learning how to compete individually as part of a team.

Students interested in trying a different kind of competitive sport should watch for additional information about upcoming tryouts.
