HPC Men’s Volleyball Makes Its Debut

By The Pointer · 10/02/26

For the first time, High Point Central is forming a men’s volleyball team.

A new athletic opportunity is coming to HPC this spring as the school prepares to form its first-ever men’s volleyball team.

Students interested in playing can attend a brief interest meeting on Wednesday, October 14, at 4:30 p.m. in the main gym.

Because the program is being established for the first time, interested students will have an opportunity to help shape the team from the beginning.

For students who have played volleyball before or those interested in trying the sport, the new team offers an opportunity to become part of a program in its first season.
